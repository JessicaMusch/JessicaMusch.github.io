Bubblr

  • Time: 48h
  • Team: 6
  • Engine: Unity
  • Platform: PC
  • Global Game Jam 2025

Bubblr was created within 48 hours during the Global Game Jam 2025 with the theme “bubble”. We worked in a group of 6, where I fulfilled the role as an assistant programmer. The goal with Bubblr was to complete the Game Jam and have a functional game at the end of our 48 hours.


Take a look at Bubblr here:
globalgamejam.org

Contributions

I worked as a programming assistant on Bubblr. I was responsible for the implementation of the UI elements, the sounds and the social media posts.
At the beginning I implemented the main menu while our lead programmer worked on the first game mechanics in the main scene. After the menu was done, I worked on implementing the posts which we included as ScriptableObjects. This task took a lot of time and after I was done, most functions were already implemented. So I worked on the in-game UI, mainly the email program and the interface of the social media platform Bubblr. In the end I implemented the sounds using the AudioManager from Unity.

About Bubblr

You're an employee at a big Social Media company. Your role is to push or censor user posts from different filter bubbles - whichever aligns with the current requirements of your boss and is beneficial to your company. By pushing or censoring posts you increase or decrease the satisfaction of your boss and the popularity of the platform. If you make both your boss and the users happy enough by managing the posts correctly, you'll be rewarded with a promotion. However, if you make him too angry or turn the platform unpopular, you'll get fired. Some posts may not align with your moral compass, but still need to get pushed because it benefits the company's agenda. With others, you may fully agree, but still have to censor them out. But be careful! Blindly pushing or censoring posts may turn the boss or the users against you...

Disclaimer: This game should be viewed as satire.

Learnings

Participating in a 48h Game Jam was new for me, and it taught me a lot about the importance of good communication in the team. We needed to communicate our process clearly throughout the day to keep track of our progress and if we needed to cut something or could continue with our original plan. Clear communication was also important to keep everyone focused, as a few hours without knowing the progress on a large part of the game had everyone agitated if it was done or if we would need to step in and help.
It also helped me to understand the importance of a good team and work environment. Multiple teams sat in one room and it helped the general moral to be able to go around, talk to people in between tasks and use the breaks to make some food together. Without this, the 48 hours would have been a lot longer and straining than they already were.

What I would change

Looking back at Fall, I would now make a few things different. Firstly, I would add a different sound effect for the acorns to let the player know that he got more points from the item. Additionally I would add a few more features to make the gameplay more interesting. Currently the player only needs to move left and right and avoid all the pumpkins. I believe that by adding a few more special items the gameplay could be enhanced without taking away the relaxing part of the game. For example: I would add another item that takes away points from the players and to counteract this, I would also add an rare item that adds a heart to the players life-counter. Also there could be other objects falling down that stay for a second or two on the ground and block the players path temporarily.
